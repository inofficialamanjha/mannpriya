An explanation of this website, the services it links to and the limits of the information you find here.

About this website

MannPriya is a static information website for Priyansi Kanakia's practice. It does not provide an account, a patient portal or a facility for submitting personal information directly to this website.

Cookies, tracking and hosting

This website does not intentionally set cookies, use analytics or advertising pixels, or embed third-party social feeds. Fonts, images and scripts used by the site are served with the website rather than requested from social networks or font services.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own practices. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for more information. This website does not control the host's processing.

Enquiry and booking services

Enquiry links lead to Google Forms. Other links may open Calendly, WhatsApp, your email application, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn or a book publisher. These are separate services with their own terms, privacy practices and possible cookies or tracking. No data is sent to those services by this website merely by displaying their links; opening a link connects you to the destination.

Before submitting a form or sending a message, review what the service asks for and share only what you are comfortable sharing. Please avoid sending detailed health information through general email or WhatsApp. You can ask Priyansi how information used in her practice is handled before beginning care.

Confidentiality in care

This website notice is not a clinical confidentiality agreement or a statement of how all practice records are managed. Confidentiality, consent, record handling and any applicable limits should be discussed directly with Priyansi before a session.

Reflections are not personalised advice

The articles and other information on this website are for general reflection and education. They are not a diagnosis, a treatment plan or a substitute for personalised assessment and care. Reading the site or sending an enquiry does not by itself establish a therapeutic relationship.

Not an emergency service

The website and its enquiry channels do not provide emergency monitoring or crisis response. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for an email, form or WhatsApp reply.

Questions

For questions about this website or to ask about the practice's information-handling arrangements, contact priyansikanakia@gmail.com.
