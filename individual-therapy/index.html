WHAT'S TAKING UP SPACE?

You can bring
the unfinished things.

Sessions can make room for what feels important to you. These are some of the experiences people may want to explore, not a checklist you need to fit.

  • Worry, stress and feeling stretched thinThe expectations you carry, the thoughts that return and the difficulty of slowing down.
  • Self-esteem and self-understandingThe way you talk to yourself, the standards you live by and what you need.
  • Loss, grief and transitionsChanges that are visible to others, and the quieter ones that can be difficult to explain.
  • Relationships and familiar patternsBoundaries, family expectations, recurring roles and your relationship with yourself.

A COLLABORATIVE CONVERSATION

Not a new version of you.
A better understanding.

Therapy is not about arriving as a polished, organised version of yourself. It can be a place to put experiences into words, notice patterns and explore what matters to you.

At the beginning, you and Priyansi can discuss your needs, expectations and what working together might involve. Suitability, session arrangements and any need for a different kind of support can be considered in that conversation.
