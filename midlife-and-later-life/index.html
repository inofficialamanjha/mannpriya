ROOM BEYOND YOUR ROLES

More than a parent, a partner, or the dependable one.

Work, family responsibilities, health concerns and loss can change what everyday life feels like. Sometimes it is a visible transition. Sometimes it is a quieter sense that your own needs have gone unheard.

Priyansi works with middle-aged and older adults. Sessions offer a place to explore what this stage of life means to you, without assuming that everyone of a similar age wants or experiences the same things.
