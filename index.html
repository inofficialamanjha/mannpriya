Have another question? ↗
01Do we need to be married to explore couples therapy?+

Relationship questions are not limited to marriage. You can enquire about support for your relationship and discuss whether the approach is a good fit for your circumstances.

02What if I do not know where to begin?+

You do not need a diagnosis, a carefully prepared story or the perfect words to make an enquiry. A simple message asking about support is enough to start the conversation.

03Are sessions available online?+

MannPriya offers online sessions and in-person support in Ahmedabad. Please enquire about current availability, location and whether your preferred format is suitable.

04How do I find out about fees and session length?+

Please ask through the enquiry form or contact channels. Priyansi can explain the current fee, session format, duration and cancellation arrangements before you decide to book.
