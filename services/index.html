WORDS THAT FEEL LIKE YOURS

You don't need
to translate yourself.

Priyansi speaks English, Gujarati and Hindi. Share your preferred language when you enquire.

Room for your whole context.

Priyansi's work includes LGBTQ+ people, gender identity, cultural and racial identity, faith, women's health and the experiences of adolescents and adults.

These are parts of a person's life and identity, not problems to be fixed. You are welcome to ask how the approach can make room for your circumstances.

AREAS OF SUPPORT

What might be
taking up space?

Priyansi works with the concerns below. You don't need to choose a label or diagnose yourself before making an enquiry.

Anxiety & mood

  • Generalized anxiety
  • Panic
  • Phobias
  • OCD
  • Depression
  • Bipolar spectrum disorders
  • Perinatal mood and anxiety disorders

Trauma & difficult experiences

  • Trauma
  • PTSD
  • Sexual, emotional and physical abuse

Habits, body & behaviour

  • Eating disorders
  • Addictions
  • Substance and alcohol use
  • Anger management
  • Behavioural weight loss

Attention & recurring patterns

  • ADHD
  • Personality disorders

Relationships, loss & wellbeing

  • Relationship issues
  • Intimacy and sexual concerns
  • Parenting concerns
  • Grief
  • Health and wellness

The right support depends on the person and their circumstances. Some concerns may also require medical, psychiatric or specialist care. Please discuss suitability before booking; this list is not a promise that every concern can be supported in the same format.
