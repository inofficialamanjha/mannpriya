A FEW PRACTICAL THINGS

Ask before
you decide.

Online sessions and in-person support in Ahmedabad are available subject to suitability and current availability.

  • Fees, format and durationAsk for the current arrangements before you book.
  • Availability and locationConfirm the appointment time and any in-person location directly with Priyansi.
  • Cancellation and reschedulingRequest the applicable policy before confirming a session.
  • Privacy and confidentialityAsk how information is handled and about the limits of confidentiality in your care.

This is not an emergency service. The website, enquiry form, email and WhatsApp are not crisis-support channels. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or visit the nearest emergency department.

External services have their own privacy practices. Read our privacy and care boundaries before sharing personal information.
