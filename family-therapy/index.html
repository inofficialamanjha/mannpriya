CHILDREN, ADOLESCENTS, PARENTS & FAMILIES

Growing isn't always easy.
For any of us.

Whether you are worried about your child, finding parenting difficult, or feeling distant as a family, there can be space to understand what is happening.

Enquire about family support ↗

MORE THAN THE MOMENT YOU SEE

Behind a difficult moment, there is a person to understand.

Children, adolescents and adults may express worries in different ways. Parenting questions and family tensions can affect everyone, even when each person experiences them differently.

Priyansi works with children, adolescents and families through a collaborative, supportive approach. An enquiry can help clarify whose needs you are seeking support for and what format may be appropriate.

A FEW POSSIBLE STARTING POINTS

Different people.
Connected lives.

  • Children & adolescentsEmotions, attention concerns, relationships and the changes that can come with growing up.
  • Parents & caregiversParenting worries, feeling stretched, or questions about how to support a child.
  • FamiliesCommunication, emotional distance, changing roles and experiences that affect life together.

BEFORE BOOKING FOR A YOUNG PERSON

Practical questions
are welcome.

Please ask about age suitability, parent or guardian involvement, consent, confidentiality and who may attend a session. These arrangements should be clarified directly rather than assumed from this website.

You can enquire in English, Gujarati or Hindi. Keep your initial message brief; there is no need to send a child's detailed personal or health history through email or WhatsApp.
