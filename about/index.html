MORE THAN A LABEL

Your story deserves
a little room.

My interest in psychology began with a wish to understand people beyond what is immediately visible. My studies in psychology began in 2016, and that curiosity continues through practice, learning and writing.

At MannPriya, I work with individual and relationship concerns. I am interested in the experiences that shape our emotional lives, the patterns we find ourselves returning to, and the meanings we make of them.

I approach these conversations with curiosity and collaboration. We can explore your experiences without rushing to decide who you should be.

WHAT GUIDES THE SPACE

Thoughtful care.
Human conversations.

Curiosity before judgement

Making space to understand your experiences in their context, rather than treating a pattern as your whole identity.

Working with you

A collaborative conversation about what matters to you, your expectations and what you would like to explore.

Room for your pace

Recognising that sharing, reflection and change do not look the same for every person or every relationship.
