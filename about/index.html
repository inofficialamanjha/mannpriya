MORE THAN A LABEL

Your story deserves
a little room.

Ever feel like you're great at meeting deadlines but not so great at meeting your own needs? Life doesn't pause outside of work. Behind the meetings and endless notifications, there may be relationship stress, parenting worries, emotional distance, or a quiet feeling of being off.

I work with individuals, couples, children, adolescents and families, as well as middle-aged and older adults. My couples work uses Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) to explore the real conversations underneath arguments or silence.

Therapy with me isn't all heavy or serious, unless it needs to be. It's thoughtful, practical, sometimes light, and always human.
